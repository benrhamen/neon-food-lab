# Pixel Chef

Kawaii pixel cooking game with a curated pantry of 77 foods, 77 original pixel drawings, and 80 learnable classic dishes.

Raw ingredients only, with these exceptions:
- Bread: white, whole-wheat, sourdough
- Pasta: penne, spaghetti, whole-wheat
- Rice: white, brown, sticky
- Noodles: egg, soba, rice, konjac (shirataki)
- Couscous
- Cheese: cheddar, mozzarella, feta, parmesan

No prepared meals, cut/preparation duplicates, tofu, yogurt, butter or oils. Food groups, search, one scrollable sectioned pantry list with a group jump bar, favourites and nutrition cards.

Recipe school: 80 classic dishes from around the world, each with a recipe lesson ("to create lasagna you need: ..."). Cook one exactly as its card says and it unlocks as a Special dish you can use in the main game. Each special dish gets its own kawaii sprite, composed per dish from its ingredients on a plate, bowl or soup base.

Dish rules: pick 1 to 10 ingredients, but only ONE Main per dish. Main, Side and Garnish roles; seated clients; rating explanations and high-scoring cooking examples.

Nutrition references: USDA SR Legacy, final 2018 release. https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets
Nutrition is per 100 g of the named reference food, not the finished dish. Pasta shapes share a plain dry pasta reference. Game scores and cooking methods are simplified play rules, not cooking or food-safety advice.

Choose 1, 3, 5 minutes or Unlimited. Music and sizzling are off until enabled. Favourites and unlocked special dishes persist on the device.

Self-contained React game, no external runtime scripts or images. The smaller pantry no longer needs DecompressionStream. Mobile-browser tested at 390 px; a physical iPhone and audible volume have not been tested.
