# Pixel Chef

Kawaii pixel cooking game with a curated pantry of 59 foods and 59 original pixel drawings.

Raw ingredients only, with these exceptions:
- Bread: white, whole-wheat, sourdough
- Pasta: penne, spaghetti, whole-wheat
- Rice: white, brown, sticky
- Noodles: egg, soba, rice
- Cheese: cheddar, mozzarella, feta, parmesan

No prepared meals, cut/preparation duplicates, tofu, yogurt, butter or oils. Food groups, search, 12-item picker pages, favourites and nutrition cards.

Nutrition references: USDA SR Legacy, final 2018 release. https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets
Nutrition is per 100 g of the named reference food, not the finished dish. Pasta shapes share a plain dry pasta reference. Game scores and cooking methods are simplified play rules, not cooking or food-safety advice.

Choose 1, 3, 5 minutes or Unlimited. Music and sizzling are off until enabled. Main, Side and Garnish roles; seated clients; rating explanations and high-scoring cooking examples.

Self-contained React game, no external runtime scripts or images. The smaller pantry no longer needs DecompressionStream. Mobile-browser tested at 390 px; a physical iPhone and audible volume have not been tested.
